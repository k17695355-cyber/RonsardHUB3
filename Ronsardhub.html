About EcoleRonsardHub

EcoleRonsardHub is an educational web-app concept designed for students studying Cambridge-style subjects from Grade 1 to Grade 12.

What's included?

  • Grade selection from 1–12
  • 8 core subjects
  • 20–42 curriculum units per subject
  • Interactive quizzes
  • Learning explanations
  • Local progress tracking
  • Dark/light mode
  • Study GPT interface

The curriculum expands automatically as the grade increases.
